## Done today

- **Design:** "Photo Booth Strip" look (cute but clean), recorded in `DESIGN.md`. The ribbon wraps a claimed gift with an animation.
- **Currency:** detected from the time zone at sign-up and guessed from a store's link (`.in` gives INR); people can always change it.
- **Phase 1, accounts and Circles:**
  - Sign in with Google (Google's own button), email and password, or an email link; password reset.
  - Welcome steps: name and currency, then start or join a Circle.
  - Circles: create one; anyone with the code or `/join/CODE` link joins straight away.
  - Circle settings: rename, code expiry, new code, admins, hand over ownership, remove, leave, delete.
  - Profile with account deletion. Circles you own pass to another member.
- **Privacy rule "Each Circle is its own room"** (`PRODUCT.md`): members never learn about your other Circles, and a gift claimed from another Circle shows only "Already claimed".
- **Phase 2, wishlists:**
  - Add wishes by pasting a link (the name is guessed from the link) or without one.
  - Each wish has a price and currency, priority hearts, occasions, notes, a photo (image link or upload shrunk to 100 KB or less), and which Circles can see it.
  - Strip and List views, filters, sorting.
  - The Circle page has a tab per member.
  - Leaving a Circle unshares your wishes from it.
- **App updates automatically** (`web/src/lib/pwa.ts`). Open tabs used to stay on the old version until closed. Now a new version takes over straight away and the page reloads into it, and the app checks for one whenever you come back to the tab and every 30 minutes.
- **Dropdowns tidied** (`.select-pill` in `web/src/index.css`): GiftHub's own plum arrow with even spacing, used on the filter and form dropdowns. On phones the filters sit in a two-by-two grid (priority and sort, then price and Strip/List). Checked at phone and desktop widths.
- **Circles page puts your Circles first** (`web/src/pages/Circles.tsx`): one card per Circle with members' initials, your role and "With Maya and Sam"; a single Circle gets one large card. Starting or joining another Circle moved below, behind two buttons ("Add another Circle").
- **GiftHub name stands out:**
  - On phones it sits on a white top bar that mirrors the bottom nav, so it never competes with the page title.
  - It is larger in the desktop side menu and on the sign-in screens.
  - `DESIGN.md` records the sizes and the rule.
- **My list fixes** (`web/src/components/ItemViews.tsx`, `web/src/lib/format.ts`):
  - A sale price's old amount now sits under the current one. It used to push the page sideways on phones and overlap the hearts in List view.
  - Whole prices drop ".00" (₹1,899).
  - The Edit buttons line up in List view.
  - The blue privacy note has "Got it" and stays hidden on that device once dismissed.
- **Link auto-fill research** (paste a link, get name, price and image) is written into Phase 4 of `Development Plan.md`. It covers what Flipkart, Myntra and Amazon.in pages contain, free fallbacks, and the first step: a Cloudflare-vs-Supabase blocking test. Amazon's PA-API references now point to its replacement, the Creators API, which needs 10 qualifying sales in 30 days.
