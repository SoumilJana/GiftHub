## Link reading: what works today

| Reads name, photo and price | Name and photo only | Not yet confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon.in, Flipkart, Shopify shops (boAt), Decathlon; Myntra, Nykaa and Ajio through scrape.do | FirstCry (Supabase retry) | Meesho, Etsy, Croma and Tata CLiQ product pages (only their home pages were tried) |

Order of tries: Cloudflare → Supabase (`parse-url`) → scrape.do (about 1,000 free credits a month, once `SCRAPEDO_TOKEN` is set) → the pre-filled form.

Safety: signed-in users only, 30 links a minute per person, no private or local addresses, 2.5 MB and 10 s per page, results cached for a day.
